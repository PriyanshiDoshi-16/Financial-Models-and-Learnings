## 2. Sector & Business Model Analysis

### Quick Commerce

Analysed the economics of the quick-commerce model, including dark-store networks, average order value, order density, fulfilment efficiency and advertising revenue. A key focus was understanding whether rapid customer acquisition and delivery speed can translate into sustainable profitability.

### FMCG

Studied the FMCG sector through its defensive characteristics, inventory turnover, distribution expansion, rural penetration, premiumization and the impact of e-commerce and quick commerce on traditional distribution models.

## 3. Investment Strategies

### AI in M&A

Explored how AI can support target identification, due diligence, financial analysis, valuation and integration planning, while recognising the continuing importance of professional judgement and decision-making.

### Leveraged Buyouts (LBO)

Studied the LBO structure, sources of value creation through margin expansion, debt paydown and multiple expansion, alongside leverage, interest-rate and default risks. Analysed cases including Blackstone–Hilton and Medline.

### Private Credit

Analysed private credit as an alternative source of corporate financing, including its growth drivers, investor rationale and key risks such as credit, liquidity, valuation, interest-rate and default risk.

### Distressed Investing

Studied a structured six-step approach to distressed investing covering opportunity identification, strategy development, due diligence, deal structuring, transaction execution and value realisation.

### Private Equity

Explored PE investment models, including venture capital, growth capital, buyouts and special situations, with emphasis on post-acquisition value creation through operational improvement, strategic changes, growth, technology adoption and capital allocation.

## 4. Corporate Strategy & Restructuring

### Demergers

Studied demergers as a strategic restructuring mechanism to improve business focus, capital allocation and valuation visibility while potentially reducing the conglomerate discount.

Analysed:

* Reliance – Jio Financial Services
* Vedanta
* Tata Motors

A key learning was that a demerger itself does not create value; value creation ultimately depends on business fundamentals, strategic clarity, capital allocation, management execution and post-separation performance.
